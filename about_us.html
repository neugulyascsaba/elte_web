About us

The Company

Founded in 2068 by propulsion engineers and orbital dynamicists, Skyrocket® began as a small nuclear-electric research firm focused on one premise: sustained thrust beats spectacle.

That philosophy was tested in 2084.

When the near-Earth asteroid FRFR-0420 entered a statistically dangerous corridor, Skyrocket® deployed its prototype nuclear-electric tug, drawing on lessons first demonstrated decades earlier by the DART mission. Instead of kinetic impact, the company used a continuous laser over two weeks to alter the asteroid's velocity enough to shift its trajectory safely away from Earth.

It was the first time a private company deliberately and measurably changed the orbit of a natural celestial body for planetary defense.

The mission proved three things:

  • Nuclear propulsion is practical.
  • Long-duration heating/pushing can outperform brute-force methods.
  • Precise orbital mechanics is the ultimate safeguard.
